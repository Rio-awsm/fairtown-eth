# S2 0.1 Implementation Plan: service skeletons, adapters, fixtures and doctor

> **For Claude:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** `services/agents` and `services/relay` start and run on mocks, FakeChain and MemoryArkivStore stand in for chain and Arkiv, the shared day fixture exists, and S2 doctor rows report (S2 0.1 in `03_BUILD_GUIDE_2_BRAINS.md`).

**Architecture:** Two Fastify 5 services sharing `@fairtown/shared`. Agents owns the `ChainAdapter` and `ArkivStore` interfaces plus their in-memory stand-ins. Design: `docs/plans/2026-11-06-s2-0.1-design.md`.

**Tech stack:** Node 22, TypeScript 5.9, Fastify 5.12 (pino built in), ws 8.22 with @types/ws, viem and zod from the catalog, Vitest 4.1, tsx.

**Rules:** Claude never commits or tags. Each "Handover" step lists the changed files and a suggested message, and the user commits. Money is always `bigint`. No keys in code, tests or fixtures: tests generate keys at runtime with `generatePrivateKey()`.

**Full test command:** `pnpm -F agents test && pnpm -F relay test`

## Deviations from the design (approved with this plan)

| # | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| P-1 | `submitBid` takes `maxPerUnit` in base units; the adapter converts it to Q96 and rounds up to the tick | An off-tick bid then can't happen; ViemChain does the same conversion. The executor still guards "clearing + tick" in step 3.1 |
| P-2 | `ArkivStore` expiry is in seconds (`expiresInSeconds`) | The SDK's `ExpirationTime` is time-based; step 2.1 maps it |

### Task 6: FakeChain, listing, bidding and settlement

**Files:** `services/agents/src/chain/fake/FakeChain.ts`, `services/agents/test/fakechain.settle.test.ts`

State, all in memory:
- Roles, balances keyed `currency:address` (including the pseudo-accounts auction, settlement and treasury), lot-token balances in 18-decimal wei, and Permit2 approvals keyed by owner, currency and spender.
- Lots, seller records, the event log, and the watch handlers.

Addresses come from `fakeAddress(tag, n)`, tx hashes from `keccak256("fake-tx-n")`, and the EIP-712 domain from `handoverDomain(chainId, settlement)`.

Rules:
- `createLot`: the caller holds a lister role and is the spec's seller; `floorPerUnit >= 1000` and `qty > 0`. Then `floorQ96 = floorToTick(toQ96(floor), tickFor(...))` and `endBlock = block + durationBlocks`. Emits `LotListed` with `toKey` bytes32 values and the currency address.
- `submitBid`: the lot is open and `block < endBlock`; the caller holds a bidder role; `maxPerUnit > clearingPerUnit`; Permit2 is approved and the balance is enough. The amount moves into the auction's escrow, and `priceQ96 = ceilToTick(toQ96(maxPerUnit), tick)`. Emits `BidSubmitted`, then `ClearingPriceUpdated` if `computeClearing` changes.
- `mine(n)`: the block counter goes up by n, `logIndex` resets, and the keeper runs. `autoMine(ms)` returns a stop function.
- **Keeper settle**, when `block >= endBlock`:
  - Run `computeClearing`. Refund `amount - paid` to each bidder and mint `units * 1e18` lot tokens. Emit `BidExited` and `TokensClaimed`.
  - Compute `splitPayout(raised, feeBps, advanceBpsFor(seller, sector))`. Fee goes to the treasury, the advance to the seller, and held stays in settlement.
  - Emit `LotSettled` with `avgPerUnit = raised / soldUnits` and `pickupDeadline = endBlock + pickupWindowBlocks`. Status becomes `sold`, or `unsold` if nothing sold.
  - Emit `TransportNeeded` for produce, fish and flowers when `soldUnits > 0`.
  - When a truck lot with a parent settles, assign the carrier: emit `CarrierAssigned` and set the parent's status to `truck_booked`.
- `advanceBpsFor`: truck sector is truck bps; proven (`marked || cleanLots >= provenAfter`) is proven bps; otherwise new bps.
- `relist`: the caller is the seller; the lot is settled with `unsoldUnits > 0` and not relisted yet; the new floor is lower. Opens a child lot with `parentLotId`, emits `LotListed` then `LotRelisted`, and sets status `relisted` if the lot was fully unsold.
- Test hooks `markProven`, `pushSignal`, `pushReference` and `pushFx` emit `SellerRecordUpdated`, `DemandSignal`, `ReferencePriceUpdated` and `FxUpdated`.

Tests (keys generated at runtime):
- **Bergen exact:** bids from the Task 5 Bergen case, then `mine(45)`.
  - `LotSettled` shows clearing and avg 9.10, sold 600, raised 5460.00, fee 54.60, sellerPaid 2702.70, held 2702.70, advance 5000.
  - Ingrid's balance is 2702.70, the losing bidder is fully refunded, and A and B each hold `300e18` tokens.
  - `TransportNeeded` qty is 600.
- **Proven:** with `markProven` set, the advance is 9000. A truck lot always gets 1000.
- **Relist:** no bids, so `soldUnits` is 0 and status `unsold`. A relist at a lower floor emits `LotListed` (parent equals the old lot) and `LotRelisted`. A higher floor throws `FloorNotLower`; a relist by a non-seller throws `NotSeller`.
- **Bid failures:** at the floor (`BelowClearing`), without Permit2 (`NoPermit2`), after `endBlock` (`AuctionEnded`), from a seller role (`CannotBid`), over the balance (`InsufficientBalance`).
- **List failures:** a buyer listing (`CannotList`), floor 999 (`FloorTooLow`).
- **Conservation:** the sum of all EUR balances (participants, treasury, settlement, auctions) equals the minted total after settlement.

### Task 7: FakeChain, tickets, carrier, delivery, paid in full

**Files:** `FakeChain.ts`, `services/agents/test/fakechain.handover.test.ts`

Rules:
- `signTicket(seller, msg)` signs with `accountFor(seller).signTypedData`. A missing key throws `NoSigner`.
- `confirmHandover(from, ticket)` checks, in order:
  1. `expiryBlock >= block`, else `TicketExpired`.
  2. The signature hasn't been used, else `TicketUsed`.
  3. `recoverTypedDataAddress` matches the seller; a throw or a mismatch is `BadSignature`.
  4. `from` is the buyer or the carrier, else `NotBuyerOrCarrier`.
  5. The buyer holds enough tokens, else `InsufficientTokens`.
  6. `heldRemaining > 0`, else `NotHeld`.
- It then burns the tokens. The release is `held * tokenAmount / soldTokens`, except the final share, which takes `heldRemaining` so the dust goes to the seller. It emits `HandoverConfirmed`.
- Status and delivery:
  - When the carrier scans the parent lot, status becomes `picked_up`.
  - When the buyer scans a truck lot that has a parent, emit `LotDelivered(parent, truckLot)` and set both lots to `delivered`.
  - Any other buyer scan emits `LotDelivered(lot, 0)` and sets status `delivered`.
- Drained, when `heldRemaining == 0`:
  - With no refunds, `cleanLots` goes up by one and `SellerRecordUpdated` is emitted.
  - `LotPaidInFull` is emitted for the lot, unless it has a carrier whose truck lot still holds money.
  - When a truck lot drains, the parent is re-checked and gets `LotPaidInFull` if it's also drained.

Tests:
- **Scene 1 chain:** a single winner, Hanse, buys 600 at 9.10. Lars lists a truck lot (parent P, 600 kg_route, floor 0.80), and Hanse bids 0.85 for 510.
  - The keeper emits `CarrierAssigned(P, T, lars)` and P becomes `truck_booked`.
  - Ingrid's ticket is confirmed by Lars: release 2702.70, P becomes `picked_up`.
  - Lars's ticket is confirmed by Hanse. The event order is `HandoverConfirmed(T)`, `LotDelivered(P, T)`, `LotPaidInFull(T)`, `LotPaidInFull(P)`.
  - Final balances: Ingrid 5405.40, Lars 504.90, treasury 59.70. Both records show `cleanLots` 1.
- **Direct pickup:** Hanse confirms, which gives `LotDelivered(P, 0)` and `LotPaidInFull(P)`.
- **Multi-buyer dust:** the second ticket releases exactly `heldRemaining`.
- **Ticket failures:** wrong signer, tampered `tokenAmount`, malformed sig, expired, reused, wrong sender (Oslo). Each throws the named code, and no balance changes.

### Task 8: FakeChain, no-show, disputes, storage

**Files:** `FakeChain.ts`, `services/agents/test/fakechain.noshow.test.ts`

Rules:
- `reportNoShow`:
  - The block must be after `pickupDeadline` (else `TooEarly`) and at most `pickupDeadline + reportWindow` (else `WindowClosed`).
  - Held money must remain (`NotHeld`) and the buyer must hold the tokens (`InsufficientTokens`). One report per buyer per lot (`AlreadyReported`).
  - It escrows the tokens, sets `contestEnds = block + contestWindow`, sets status `no_show_reported` and emits `NoShowReported`.
- `contestNoShow`: the caller is the seller and `block <= contestEnds`. It emits `DisputeOpened` with kind 1 and `NoShowContested`, and status becomes `disputed`.
- **Keeper finalize**, when `block > contestEnds` and the report is uncontested:
  - Refund the buyer's share of held money, emit `NoShowRefunded`, and set status `refunded`.
  - Update the record: `noShows` goes up by one, `cleanLots` resets to 0, `proven` and the operator mark clear. Emit `SellerRecordUpdated`.
- **Keeper release-expired**, after `pickupDeadline + reportWindow` with no open report or dispute: emit `HeldReleased`, mark the lot drained (clean and paid in full).
- `openDispute`: a buyer, within the report window, holding the tokens. It escrows them, emits `DisputeOpened` with kind 0, and status becomes `disputed`.
- `resolveRuling(disputeId, refundBps)` is a test hook for W3:
  - Split the buyer's share: `refundBps` of it to the buyer, the rest to the seller. Emit `RulingSettled` and `DisputeResolved`.
  - A kind 1 dispute at 10000 bps applies the no-show penalty.
  - Status becomes `refunded` if anything was refunded, otherwise the lot is drained.
- `requestStorage`: the caller holds `qty * 1e18` tokens; emit `StorageNeeded`.

Tests:
- **Uncontested no-show:**
  - Reporting at `pickupDeadline` throws `TooEarly`. One block later it succeeds.
  - After `mine(60)` nothing has happened yet; after one more block, `NoShowRefunded` refunds 2702.70.
  - Hanse ends with `10000 - 5460 + 2702.70`. Ingrid's record shows `noShows` 1, `cleanLots` 0, not proven.
  - A proven seller loses the mark, so their next lot settles at 5000.
- **Contested:** after the contest, `mine(100)` produces no refund. `resolveRuling(id, 0)` pays the seller and the lot is clean. A contest after the window throws `WindowClosed`.
- **Silent buyer:** past the report window, the keeper emits `HeldReleased` (2702.70) and `LotPaidInFull`. A late report throws `WindowClosed`.
- **Quality dispute at 2500 bps:** Hanse gets 675.675 back, Ingrid gets 2027.025, status is `refunded`, and `cleanLots` doesn't go up.
- **Storage:** `requestStorage` for 600 emits `StorageNeeded`; for 601 it throws `InsufficientTokens`.
- **Conservation** holds after every path.
